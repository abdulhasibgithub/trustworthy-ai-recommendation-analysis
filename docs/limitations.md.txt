# Limitations of the AI Recommendation Analysis Workflow

This document summarizes the limitations of the exploratory workflow presented in this repository.

---

## 1. Non-Independence of AI Outputs

- Recommendations from AI systems within the same family may be correlated.  
- Dependence-aware weighting reduces, but does not fully eliminate, this bias.  
- Results should be interpreted with this consideration in mind.

---

## 2. Heuristic Weighting and Diversity Bonuses

- Base weights for recommendation categories (`Hit`, `FreshHit`, `Final`, `FreshFinal`) are heuristic.  
- The diversity bonus is a simple log-based adjustment to reward cross-model support.  
- These schemes are not formally validated statistical methods.

---

## 3. Small Sample Size

- The dataset of AI-generated recommendations is limited and exploratory.  
- Quantitative results may not generalize to larger or different AI outputs.

---

## 4. Exploratory Nature

- The workflow is **not intended for formal project selection**.  
- Outputs demonstrate **reproducible methodology** and **analytical reasoning**, but not authoritative decision-making.

---

## 5. Transparency and Interpretability Emphasis

- All scripts include explicit interpretation and session information.  
- This emphasizes methodological awareness over predictive accuracy.  
- Users should treat the results as **illustrative and reproducible exploratory analysis**.

---

## 6. Summary

- This repository demonstrates reproducible, dependence-aware aggregation of heterogeneous AI recommendations.  
- Limitations are inherent to exploratory computational workflows and heuristic design choices.  
- Users and reviewers should consider the results as methodological demonstration rather than final decision outputs.
